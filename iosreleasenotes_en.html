v1.9.9 2026-10

  • New import template library: ready-made templates for Shopee, Shopline, Lazada, TikTok Shop, Shopify, Amazon and more. The app suggests a template when it recognises a report, the library updates online, and “Check for updates” gets the latest templates right away.
  • Major custom-import upgrades: configure headers, skipped rows, date order, fill-down, first-sheet fallback, one item per row, and multi-step product short names, with summaries, name-collision warnings, regex testing, and AI prompts.
  • Broader file compatibility: enter passwords for protected Excel files at import; CSV / TXT files in UTF-8, Big5, GBK, Shift_JIS and EUC-KR; dates with English month names (such as 03 Jun 2026).
  • Better product specifications and short names: Free / Lite now keep specifications; variants of a product can share a short name, which is kept by product code after a rename.
  • Voice and headsets: a Bluetooth headset's microphone works for voice commands, which keep working after switching to another app; the headset “complete” command moves on to the next unfinished group, and listening resumes after a voice reprint.
  • Bluetooth label printers: fixed labels occasionally going missing when printing one after another.
  • Interface: the font scale now applies to tabs, titles and menus; order management gains copy, edit and delete; fixed the bottom buttons sitting in the wrong place after the iPad rotates in the background.
  • Subscriptions and help: plan prices and billing periods follow your App Store region, and restoring purchases shows its result; the help center's product demo card comes first and offers scenario or how-to videos.

v1.9.5 2026-10

  • SortingGo for iOS is here: runs on iPhone and iPad (iOS 26 or later) with the same features as SortingGo for Android v1.9.5.
  • Order import: Excel / CSV, custom import formats and password-protected Excel files, picked from the device, an SMB / NAS folder in the Files app, or SFTP.
  • Sorting work: putting and picking, allocation and preparation, with spoken prompts (including storage locations), voice control and headset buttons.
  • Label printing through SortingGo Print Service or a Bluetooth label printer.
  • Route sequence templates, product short names, Excel result export and the WarehousingGo connection.
  • Nine languages, a built-in help center and interactive tutorials; subscriptions are purchased and managed through the App Store.
