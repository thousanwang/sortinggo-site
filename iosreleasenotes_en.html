v1.9.5 2026-10

  • SortingGo for iOS is here: runs on iPhone and iPad (iOS 26 or later) with the same features as SortingGo for Android v1.9.5.
  • Order import: Excel / CSV, custom import formats and password-protected Excel files, picked from the device, an SMB / NAS folder in the Files app, or SFTP.
  • Sorting work: putting and picking, allocation and preparation, with spoken prompts (including storage locations), voice control and headset buttons.
  • Label printing through SortingGo Print Service or a Bluetooth label printer.
  • Route sequence templates, product short names, Excel result export and the WarehousingGo connection.
  • Nine languages, a built-in help center and interactive tutorials; subscriptions are purchased and managed through the App Store.
